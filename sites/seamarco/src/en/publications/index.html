SEAMARCO distributes knowledge on marine animals in the following ways:

  • Publications in scientific journals.
  • Publications in books and chapters.
  • Lectures at scientific conferences and symposia.
  • Participation in workshops.
  • Presentations for funders of studies conducted by SEAMARCO.
  • Lectures at meetings organized by environmental organizations.
  • Guest lectures in higher education settings.
  • Seminars of board members and personnel of government agencies.
  • Interviews with the media (radio, TV, newspapers, and magazines).
